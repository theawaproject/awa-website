About the event

Young women are increasingly invited into multilateral water processes, but they are rarely the ones shaping the outcomes. In this 90-minute session, speakers from South Asia, Latin America and across the Global South share how they have moved agendas and where they have been blocked. Your ideas, gathered live during the session, will feed a joint policy op-ed with recommendations for multilateral institutions.

Open to everyone. Young women lead the conversation; allies and institutions are welcome to listen and commit.
