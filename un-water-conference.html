From Presence to Influence: Young Women Reshaping Multilateral Water Decisions

Young women are increasingly invited into multilateral water processes, yet being in the room does not mean shaping what leaves it. Join young women from the Global South to explore what separates presence from influence.

About the event

A seat in the room is not the same as a voice in the outcome

This session centres young women from the Global South, with an intersectional lens that brings in Indigenous young women and the wider youth constituency. Speakers from South Asia, Latin America and other Global South regions will share how they have moved agendas, and where they have been blocked.

An interactive segment will capture participants’ own barriers and proposals in real time. Everything we gather feeds a co-authored policy op-ed with concrete recommendations for multilateral institutions.

Young women lead the conversation, and everyone is welcome: institutional representatives, senior practitioners and allies are invited to listen and commit.

Young women do not need only a seat in the room; they need fair access to the knowledge, networks, resources and decision-making pathways that allow their expertise to shape outcomes.

What we’ll explore

Six questions for the session

  • How expertise from economics, food security, climate, health, biodiversity and diplomacy strengthens water decisions.
  • The difference between being invited into a process and being able to change its direction.
  • The barriers young women from the Global South face, from visas and funding to language and the recognition of traditional knowledge.
  • Strategies that have helped young women navigate and influence multilateral spaces.
  • Practical recommendations for multilateral institutions, networks and emerging professionals.
  • How to keep the conversation going after the Conference.
