Pathway to Leadership

Your path to leadership in water action

At AWA, you grow through real contribution, not just time served. Every volunteer has a clear path from getting started to leading entire programs.

1

Fellow

Where everyone starts. Learn how AWA works, get hands-on, and find your footing.

2

Associate

Take ownership of your own piece of the work, with more independence and trust.

3

Coordinator

Lead an entire project or workstream largely on your own.

4

Manager

Shape strategy for a whole area of AWA's work, not just your own projects.

5

Director

Own the vision, the people, and the results for a whole part of the organization.

Moving up takes real experience, not just time. Each level has a minimum time in role, but reaching it is the starting point for the next step, not a guarantee.

A Note on Wellbeing

Advocacy takes a toll. We take that seriously.

Through our partnership with the Mental Health Youth Alliance, AWA runs sessions specifically for young changemakers navigating climate anxiety, because sustainable leadership requires personal resilience too.
