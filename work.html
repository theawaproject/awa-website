Our Work

Three pillars. One mission.

Advocacy, community action, and access to information, working together to build a water-just world.

Our Approach

Water justice, from policy tables to village wells

We believe water is more than an economic resource. It's a matter of culture, gender equality, environment, and community. Our work sits at the intersection of three pillars, each reinforcing the others.

01

Advocacy & Representation

Elevating youth voices, especially from Indigenous and underrepresented communities, in national and international water governance forums.

Initiatives

Where the work actually happens

Six formats carry AWA's mission into the field, onto the airwaves, and into the rooms where policy gets made.

Youth Leadership

The Ambassador Program

Our flagship pathway for young people to step into water advocacy: building skills, leading local projects, and joining AWA's global network of youth change-makers.

Podcast

Water Voices Around the World

Conversations with youth leaders, researchers, and communities confronting water scarcity across the globe. Twelve episodes and counting, with listenership growing every year.

Community Projects

Hands-On Field Work

Practical, on-the-ground solutions like our fog-harvesting nets piloted in Peru's coastal desert, putting innovation directly into communities' hands.

Research

Youth Water Governance Studies

In-depth research like our study in San Juan de Marcona, Ica, combining survey data with real conversations about the solutions communities actually want.
